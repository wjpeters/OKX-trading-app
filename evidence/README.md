# Verification evidence

## Public evidence and local account data

This repository is public. Personal account quantities and balance calculations are omitted from the published chart-price and live-balance browser/local reports. Original reports are retained locally under `.local/`, outside Git. Screenshots `live-balances-assets.jpg` and `live-balances-terminal.jpg` show personal live balances and are also excluded from Git.

All other source, documentation and publishable verification artifacts are included. The neural-network export files prefixed `TEST-` contain synthetic test weights and do not establish historical qualification, forward acceptance, Demo acceptance or trading permission.
